Destination: Visakhapatnam
Category: Coastal & Attractions
Title: The City of Destiny - Coastal Beaches & Submarines

INS Kursura Submarine Museum & RK Beach:
INS Kursura is a Soviet-era submarine museum positioned on RK Beach sands in Visakhapatnam. Visitors can walk through the interior compartments to learn about naval life, torpedo tubes, sonar control rooms, and submarine operations.

Kailasagiri Hill & Cable Car:
Kailasagiri is a hilltop park offering panoramic views of the Bay of Bengal coastline and the city of Vizag. Features massive white statues of Lord Shiva and Parvathi, a circular toy train, and a scenic cable-car ropeway ride.

Rushikonda Beach & Water Sports:
Rushikonda Beach is famous for blue flag certified waters, golden sands, surfing, jet skiing, and sea kayaking along the Eastern Ghats hills backdrop.
