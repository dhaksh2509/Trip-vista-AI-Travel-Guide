Destination: Bengaluru
Category: Garden City & Tech Capital
Title: Garden City - Palaces, Botanical Gardens & Craft Breweries

Lalbagh Botanical Garden & Cubbon Park:
Spanning 240 acres, Lalbagh features a glass house modeled on London's Crystal Palace and a 3-billion-year-old rock outcrop. Cubbon Park provides 300 acres of lush green cover in the city heart.

Bengaluru Palace & Tipu Sultan's Summer Palace:
Bengaluru Palace displays Tudor-style architecture with fortified towers, carved woodwork, and oil paintings. Tipu Sultan's Summer Palace is an ornate teakwood structure built in 1791.

South Indian Dosa & Craft Beer:
Iconic eateries like MTR (Mavalli Tiffin Room) and Vidyarthi Bhavan serve golden butter masale dosas and filter coffee, alongside Bengaluru's vibrant microbrewery scene in Indiranagar and Koramangala.
