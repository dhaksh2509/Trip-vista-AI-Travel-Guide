Destination: Goa
Category: Beaches & Colonial Heritage
Title: Sun, Sand & Portuguese Heritage

Fort Aguada & Old Goa Churches:
Goa blends 17th-century Portuguese coastal forts like Fort Aguada with 4-story lighthouses overlooking the Arabian Sea, alongside UNESCO World Heritage churches like Basilica of Bom Jesus containing the sacred relics of St. Francis Xavier.

Baga Beach & Water Sports:
Baga and Calangute beaches feature lively shacks, parasailing, banana rides, jet skiing, and vibrant night markets along North Goa.

South Goa Serenity:
Palolem and Agonda beaches offer serene palm-fringed bays, silent disco nights, yoga retreats, and pristine coastal estuaries.
