Destination: Delhi
Category: Capital Heritage & Street Food
Title: Historic Capital - Mughal Monuments & Street Gastronomy

Red Fort, Qutub Minar & Humayun's Tomb:
Delhi spans centuries of imperial dynasties, from Mughal red sandstone fortresses to the 73-meter Qutub Minar brick minaret and Humayun's Tomb, the architectural predecessor to the Taj Mahal.

Chandni Chowk Street Food:
Chandni Chowk offers vibrant culinary lanes including Paranthe Wali Gali, crisp jalebis at Old Famous Jalebi Wala, and savory chaat at Natraj Dahi Bhalle.
