Destination: Hyderabad
Category: History, Heritage & Food
Title: City of Pearls - Nizam Royal Heritage & Hyderabadi Biryani

Charminar & Laad Bazaar:
Built in 1591 by Sultan Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah, Charminar features four grand arches and 56-meter minarets. Surrounding Charminar is Laad Bazaar, renowned for traditional handcrafted lac bangles, pearls, and zari embroidery.

Golconda Fort & Light & Sound Show:
Golconda Fort is a monumental medieval fortress complex known for acoustic marvels (a handclap at the entrance gate resonates at the top citadel 1 km away), royal palaces, and diamond vaults where the Koh-i-Noor diamond was stored.

Hyderabadi Dum Biryani & Irani Chai:
World-famous Hyderabadi Dum Biryani combines long-grain basmati rice cooked on slow dum fire with fragrant spices and tender marinated meat. Accompanied by Irani chai and Osmania biscuits at iconic cafes like Nimrah and Paradise.
