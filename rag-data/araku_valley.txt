Destination: Araku Valley
Category: Attractions & Food
Title: Top Attractions & Sightseeing in Araku Valley

Borra Caves (One Million Years Old):
Located along the Ananthagiri hills, Borra Caves are prehistoric limestone structures situated at 705 meters above sea level. Featuring striking stalactites and stalagmites shaped naturally over millennia, the cave reaches depths of up to 80 meters. The Gosthani River flows through these caverns. Well illuminated with colorful LED lighting, the caves require about 1.5 to 2 hours to explore. Entry ticket costs ₹80 per adult.

Katiki Waterfalls & 4x4 Jeep Safari:
Originating from the Gosthani River, Katiki Waterfalls plunges 50 feet into a crystal clear pool surrounded by lush Eastern Ghats greenery. Reaching Katiki requires taking a local 4x4 jeep ride from Borra Caves (approx. 7 km over rocky hill terrain costing ₹250-₹350 per seat) followed by a 20-minute uphill trek through verdant forest.

Araku Tribal Museum & Coffee Museum:
Situated in Araku town center, the Tribal Museum showcases traditional mud huts, lifelike clay figures of indigenous tribes, and authentic handicrafts. Just across the road lies the Araku Coffee Museum, celebrating world-renowned organically grown Arabica beans with tasting demos.

Food & Cuisine:
Bongu Lo Kodi (Bamboo Chicken): Bongu Chicken is the quintessential tribal culinary masterpiece of Araku. Tender chicken is marinated with organic wild ginger, garlic, green chilies, and local forest spices without any oil or water, stuffed inside green bamboo stalks, and charcoal roasted for 40 minutes.
